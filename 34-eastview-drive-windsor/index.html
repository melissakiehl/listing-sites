Why Buyers Will
Love Life in Windsor

01

A riverfront park and Riverwalk, with construction starting in December

Riverfront Recapture's Hartford-Windsor line park project is planned to include a 19-acre cove for paddling and a Riverwalk linking Riverside Park north to Windsor Meadow State Park and the East Coast Greenway. Construction across the riverfront property is set to begin in December 2026.

02

A new tenant for the historic Carriage House on Broad Street

On November 16, 2026, the Town Council unanimously approved a three-year lease of the Luddy Carriage House at 261 Broad Street, next to Town Hall, for a fashion boutique, so the historic building in Windsor Center will have an active shop.

03

A Vietnam War Memorial planned for the town green

On November 10, 2026, the Planning and Zoning Commission voted 5-0 to recommend a black granite Vietnam War Memorial for the north end of the Windsor town green, with the town hoping to have it installed by May 2027. The Town Council decides next.
